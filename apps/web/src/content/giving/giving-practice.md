# Giving — Daily Reading
*Read a little each day. The secret of living is giving.*

> The fastest way to get what you want in life — and to feel steady peace and joy — is to give. Give the right way, and health, wealth, and happiness follow.

## Why giving works
Two simple truths sit under everything:
- **The source is unlimited.** Your good doesn't really come from one job, one client, or one person — it flows from life itself (the Universe / God), which has an endless supply. So you never have to fear "there won't be enough."
- **What you give comes back — multiplied.** This is the law of sowing and reaping (also called karma, or the law of attraction). Plant corn, get corn. Plant love, get love. Plant money, reap money. Whatever you send out returns to you, larger and stronger.

**Example:** say a kind word and you plant a seed for kindness to come back to you — often many times over. Say a hateful one, and that returns too. So choose your seeds on purpose.

**Tip:** if you're not consciously giving, you're not planting good seeds — and a field with no seeds grows weeds. Give every day.

## What to give
You can give in four ways — **words, thoughts, deeds, and things** — and every gift also carries your **thoughts** (your attitude and feeling). Thoughts come first, and they matter most, because your feeling is the real "signal" you send out.

- **Words** — a compliment, encouragement, a thank-you.
- **Thoughts** — silently wishing someone well, praying for them.
- **Deeds** — your time, help, teaching, volunteering.
- **Things** — money, food, a gift, something you own.

Two rules of thumb:
- **Give what's hardest for you.** For most people that's **money**, because it feels like giving away a piece of yourself. That's exactly why it works so well.
- **Give what you want to receive.** Want more money? Give money. Want love? Give love. Want respect? Give respect.

**Example:** quietly praying for a friend is a gift of both a deed (your time) and thoughts (your care) — even if they never know.

## How much to give
Give enough that you **feel a little sacrifice — but with joy.** That mix is what produces the surprising returns.
- If giving money means cutting back on something small, that little sting is the sacrifice that makes it powerful.
- If giving your time means missing your favorite show, that counts too.

**Stay sensible:** don't go into debt or empty the money you need for bills. Give to the level of your **faith** — your calm certainty that all will be well — but don't be reckless.

**A simple measure many traditions use:**
- **Tithe — 10%.** Give 10% of your income (after tax is fine) to whoever "feeds" you spiritually, mentally, or emotionally. Give it **first**, off the top, before anything else.
- **Offerings — above 10%.** Anything beyond the tithe is where the real magic lives. The tithe blesses you; the extra creates the surprises.

**Tip:** treat your giving like the first bill you pay, not the leftover.

## Who to give to
- **Not** to someone just because you expect them to pay you back — your return usually comes from a completely different direction.
- Give where you **feel drawn** — a mentor, a teacher, a cause, a charity, a place of worship, someone who helped you.
- **First, feed those who feed you** — the people or place that nourish your soul, spirit, and mind.

**Example:** you might give to your teacher, but your "harvest" shows up months later as an unexpected bonus at work. Don't try to predict the channel.

## Your attitude (the key)
This is what makes giving actually work:
- Be a **cheerful, joyful giver** — no fear, no nervousness, no sense of loss.
- Remember it was **never really yours** anyway — you're just passing along what life lent you.
- Feel that **it's more blessed to give than to receive** — you gain more by giving.
- See that **we're all one** — in a way, you're giving to life, and to yourself.
- Give **for the joy of giving first**, and let the harvest be a bonus.

**Tip:** if you give with fear or resentment, you plant fear and resentment. Give only with a light, glad heart.

## Expect a return
Give knowing a harvest **will** come — just like a farmer plants seeds fully expecting a crop. Each morning you can tell yourself: **"Something good is going to happen to me today, because I am a cheerful giver."**

## …then let go of the "how"
Expect a return — but **release** any grip on **how or when** it shows up. Surrender the details to life; it may have a far better plan than you'd script.
- Don't obsess over the exact payoff or timing.
- Stay open and relaxed, in a state of "allowing."
- **Feel good now**, trusting that all is well and you can't get it wrong.

**Example:** one man gave away every last penny he had, bills looming. No money arrived in time — but when he called his creditors, each said his account was already paid ahead, and his landlord even *lowered* his rent. The return came, just not the way he pictured it.

## Give in secret
Give so that **few — ideally no one — know.** Don't announce it or brag about it. When only you (and the Universe) know, the reward comes back to you openly. (Sharing quietly with a spouse who's fully on board is fine.)

## Give thanks — as if it's already done
After you give, take a quiet moment to **thank life for the harvest as though you've already received it.** It's not begging — it's grateful appreciation, in advance. Feel the joy and gratitude now, not "someday."

## Forgive
Finally, let go of any grudge. Send **love and forgiveness** to anyone you still hold ill feelings toward — forgive them completely, and even ask their forgiveness for holding on so long. Releasing that negativity clears the way for your blessings to flow.

## Your daily giving practice
A simple routine you can run every day:
1. **Give something** — words, thoughts, deeds, or things. When you can, give what's hardest (often money), and give what you want more of.
2. **Give first, cheerfully, and in secret** — off the top, with a glad heart, quietly.
3. **Feel a little sacrifice, stay sensible** — enough to matter, never into debt.
4. **Expect the harvest**, then **release the "how."**
5. **Give thanks** as if it's already arrived.
6. **Forgive** someone, and send them love.

Do this daily, and watch the results unfold.
